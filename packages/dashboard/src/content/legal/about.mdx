# About MuiRouter

> One API key for every LLM. No VPN. No subscription. Pay per token.

## Vision

Large language models are diverging faster than any single team can integrate. MuiRouter exists to be the **boring, stable routing layer** underneath your product:

- **One key, every provider.** OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Grok, MiMo and more behind a single OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
- **Edge-native.** Proxied through Cloudflare's 300+ PoPs so the route is close to your users, not your VPN.
- **Pay for what you burn.** No seats, no bundles, no expiring credits. Top up, call, inspect `GET /v1/usage` — that is the whole billing model.
- **MCP-native.** The same router speaks Model Context Protocol over streamable HTTP, so Claude Code, Cursor, Cline and any MCP client can reuse the same key and wallet.
- **Infrastructure for our own products.** MuiRouter was built to be the foundation for the AI products Meathill Studio ships next. If it is reliable enough for us and our friends, it is reliable enough for you.

## Who builds it

MuiRouter is operated by **Meathill LLC**, a small studio behind `muirouter.com`. Engineering, support and billing are handled by the same team that ships code — `support@muirouter.com` reaches a human.

## What we optimize for

Stability over novelty, transparency over bundling, and a bill you can explain to your accountant.
